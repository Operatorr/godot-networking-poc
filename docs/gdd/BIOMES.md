# Biomes

Three distinct worlds with with biomes

- Mainland (Tier 1 to 4)
  - Forest / Meadows (Tier 1)
  - Beach (Tier 1)
  - Dark Forest / Deep Forest / Dense Forest (Tier 2)
  - Reef (Tier 3)
  - Mountains (Tier 3)
  - Ocean (Tier 4)
  - Unholy / Infected (Outside) (Tier 4)
- Underworld (Tier 3 to 7)
  - Rocks (Tier 3)
  - Scorched Ground (Tier 4)
  - Mountain (Tier 4)
  - Lava (Tier 5)
  - Purgatory (Tier 6)
  - Hell (Tier 7)
- The Creators Realm (Tier 4 to 7)
  - Grass (Tier 4)
  - Cloud (Tier 5)
  - Platform (Tier 5)
  - Construct (Tier 6)
  - Void (Tier 7)
  - Higher Construct (Tier 7)
