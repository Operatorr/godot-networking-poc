Omega Realm is a brutal top-down multiplayer shooter set in a dying world consumed by eldritch corruption. Descend through cursed realms, battle nightmarish horrors, and stake your claim in a land where permadeath turns every fight into a desperate gamble for survival.

---

## Theme & Atmosphere

### A World Without Hope

There is no salvation coming. The great empires have fallen, and their armies fight on anyway, long after anyone remembers why. The gods are silent, or worse, they are listening. What remains is a world ground down by endless war: trench lines that stretch past the horizon, fortresses that change hands a hundred times, and generations born only to be spent. Humanity clings to existence not out of hope, but out of spite.

### Grimdark Fantasy Meets Cosmic Horror

This is a world where cruelty is law and suffering is policy. Fanatical war-cults march millions into the grinder against horrors that make their zealotry seem reasonable. The enemies you face are not merely dangerous. They are wrong. Geometries that fracture sanity. Entities older than stars, for whom your extinction is not malice but indifference. Against such things there are no good sides, only sides that are still standing. Faith is not comfort here. It is ammunition.

### Life is Cheap. Death is Cheaper.

No hand-holding. No safety nets. In Omega Realm, you earn every level, every piece of gear, and every breath. The strongest warriors carry scars from a hundred battles. Those who don't carry graves. There are no heroes here. Only survivors, and even they are living on borrowed time.

---

## Core Fantasy

**"I survived what killed others."**

Players come to Omega Realm for the primal thrill of high-stakes combat. Every dungeon run could be your last. Every loot drop could be your salvation, or the prize someone murders you for.

---

## Gameplay Pillars

### Bullet Hell Combat

Weave through cascading patterns of eldritch projectiles. Split-second reflexes separate the living from the dead.

### Permadeath Stakes

Hardcore mode means permanent death. Your legend lives on the leaderboard. Your character does not.

### Cutthroat Competition

Races and tournaments with real consequences. Will you cooperate with others, or put a knife in their back when they least expect it?

### Fast Progression, Deep Endgame

Race to level cap. Then the real game begins: hunting rare gear, consuming permanent stat potions, and climbing the ladder.

## Visual Identity

This is a pixel art game.

**Important: 3/4 top-down oblique perspective.**

- **Color Palette:** Oppressive blacks, dried-blood browns, rusted iron reds, tarnished gold, and cold bone white, punctuated by the sickening glow of eldritch corruption in violets and void-blues
- **Environment:** Brutalist war-fortresses and bunker-shrines, scarred by centuries of siege. Industrial hellscapes of iron, smoke and ash. Trench networks, razor wire, and cratered no-man's-land. Mass graves and trophy walls built from the bones of the fallen. Dimensional wounds leaking impossible geometries into a world already drowning in war
- **Enemies:** Corrupted soldiers fused to their armor and still following orders no one gave. Daemon-touched abominations that shatter sanity on sight. Shambling masses of the once-faithful, conscripted even in death. Cosmic entities so vast and indifferent that humanity is merely an afterthought in their unknowable designs
- **Effects:** Weighty, punishing impacts: blood sprays, bone shatters, the grinding of metal against metal. Eldritch magic tears reality itself, leaving wounds in the air that whisper of extinction
- **Atmosphere:** Perpetual twilight. Ash falls like snow. Every surface tells a story of attrition: purity seals nailed to armor, kill-tallies scratched into walls, bodies left where they fell because no one is left to bury them, war banners defaced by madness. Hope is a fading memory. Faith is the only weapon left. Mercy is weakness
