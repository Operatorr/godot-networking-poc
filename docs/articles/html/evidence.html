Evidence rules the drafts follow

  • Results cite only the accepted bounded results audit (author-accepted 2026-10-25), under the bounded correctness closure. No complete clean campaign, general capacity limit, 500–1000-player result, long-term stability or causal speedup percentage is claimed.
  • Two builds, never pooled.
  • Original build: game b1266c1, 14 Oct 2026 campaign, 512 KiB effective socket buffers. Source of the five failed Strategy ramps (191/185/176/180/190), the original ladder, Strategy 50/100 transfer.
  • Fixed build: game SHA-256 de92e120…, 22–24 Oct 2026 correctness campaign, 4 MiB effective game buffers. Source of the ten clean cells, including Strategy 150. Its extracted fixes on master were validated locally, not load-tested again.
  • Failed and invalid classifications stand: the ramps are failed join ramps, not a capacity; Idle150 is non-clean; fixed-build Strategy200 is collector-invalid; Strategy125/150 (original) are generator-invalid. Unknowns stay unknown.
  • The wire-format byte breakdown comes from a local 50-bot tally on commit 62fbfc3, before the correctness fixes. Layouts didn't change, so bit costs stand; byte shares are marked indicative.
  • The "14.85 KB/s vs 5 KB/s" sidebar from the prototype outline is cut, as decided. The 32.9 Hz estimator story appears only as an optional "number that lied" sidebar, using the fixed estimator's behaviour, which the correctness work supports.
  • Industry numbers carry the research notes' confidence tags: [P] primary, [S] first-party via press or mirror, [U] unverified (mostly cut), [O] observed third-party, [D] our arithmetic. Every [D] is reproduced in Appendix A.
